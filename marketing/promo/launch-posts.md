## LinkedIn

I built a drum machine that runs in your browser.

It's called audx. You click steps into a grid, set the tempo and swing, mute and solo tracks, and chain patterns into a song. When it sounds right, you can download a WAV or a zip of stems. If you have a MIDI controller, you can play it from that in Chrome.

The beat in this video is a UK garage 2-step at 132 BPM, and audx sequenced every part of it.

There's nothing to install. It's open source (MIT) and still early, so if you try it and something breaks, tell me.

Open the studio: https://audx-five.vercel.app/studio
Code: https://github.com/chrisschouk/audx
